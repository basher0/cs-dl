Jail_Andaman

- Map for Jailbreak/Prisonbreak mod.
- Map works for CS 1.6 and CZ.
- Contains custum wads.
- Made with special mapping techniques and advanced functions.

Installation tips
- Copy all files into your server's cstrike filder in the same structure as in the ZIP file.
- In order to keep all terrorists inside the cells untill CT's open it in the beginning of each round,
  go to your server.cfg and change CVar "mp_freezetime" to about 3-5.

!!NOTE!!
Please do not change anything, for example filenames and RES entries,
in order to avoid download problems for yourself and other players.
